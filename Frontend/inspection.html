The trained ResNet50 model contains six classes representing the available metal types and surface conditions in the current dataset.

01 Aluminium — Defective
02 Aluminium — Good
03 Copper — Defective

05 Iron — Defective

Important: The current trained dataset does not contain verified individual defect labels such as rust, scratch or crack. Defective predictions are therefore reported as Surface Defect.
